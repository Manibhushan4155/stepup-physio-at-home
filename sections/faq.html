Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers For Home Physiotherapy

Everything you need to know about home-visit sessions, recovery plans, and care with Dr. Shashi Bhushan Kumar.

1. What is Step Up Physio At Home?

Step Up Physio At Home is a dedicated home-visit physiotherapy and rehabilitation service led by Dr. Shashi Bhushan Kumar (PT), holding B.P.T and M.P.T (Neuro) qualifications. Instead of travelling in traffic or waiting at a clinic, patients receive focused, one-on-one clinical care right in the comfort and safety of their own home.

Every treatment plan is tailored to the patient's daily home environment, mobility goals, and medical history so recovery feels practical, consistent, and stress-free for both the patient and family members.

2. What conditions do you treat at home?

We provide structured home rehabilitation across six core speciality areas:

  • Neurological Rehabilitation: Stroke recovery, Parkinsonism, balance and gait training, muscle weakness, and post-neurological surgery care.
  • Orthopaedic Rehabilitation: Lower back pain, cervical/neck pain, arthritis, frozen shoulder, joint stiffness, and musculoskeletal pain.
  • Post-Surgical Rehabilitation: Recovery after Total Knee Replacement (TKR), Total Hip Replacement (THR), fractures, and orthopaedic procedures.
  • Certified Cupping & Manual Therapy: Myofascial decompression and hands-on joint/soft-tissue mobilization for tight muscles and chronic stiffness.
  • Geriatric Physiotherapy: Senior mobility, strength building, balance improvement, and fall-prevention programs at home.
  • Ergonomic Training & Home Care: Posture correction, workstation setup guidance, and safe daily movement training.
3. How does a home physiotherapy session work?

Booking and starting your session is simple and transparent:

  1. Book Your Slot: Submit your details, condition, preferred date/time slot, and map location on the website or connect directly on WhatsApp/Call.
  2. Detailed Assessment: During the first visit, pain levels, joint range of motion, muscle strength, balance, and previous medical reports are evaluated thoroughly.
  3. Hands-On Therapy & Guided Exercises: Targeted manual therapy, mobility drills, and supervised exercises are performed at a comfortable pace.
  4. Home Routine & Progress Tracking: Clear daily exercise guidance is shared with the patient and caregivers so progress continues steadily between visits.
4. Is home physiotherapy suitable for elderly patients?

Yes, it is especially beneficial for elderly patients. Senior citizens often experience fatigue, joint pain, post-hospital weakness, or balance issues that make frequent clinic visits tiring or risky.

Home sessions allow therapy directly on the bed, chair, stairs, and rooms the elderly person uses every day—helping improve walking confidence, joint flexibility, and fall prevention in a familiar, supportive environment with family present.
