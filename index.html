FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Step Up Physio At Home?

Step Up Physio At Home is a professional home-visit physiotherapy service led by Dr. Shashi Bhushan Kumar (PT), providing personalized, evidence-based rehabilitation across Noida and Delhi.

2. What conditions do you treat at home?

We treat Neck Pain, Back Pain, Knee Pain, Shoulder Pain, Frozen Shoulder, Postoperative Rehabilitation (TKR, THR), Orthopaedic injuries, Neurological conditions (Stroke, Parkinsonism), Ergonomic posture issues, and Geriatric mobility problems.

3. How does a home physiotherapy session work?

Dr. Shashi visits your home with necessary portable therapy equipment, assesses your physical condition and movement goals, delivers hands-on manual/cupping/electrotherapy, and guides you through a tailored exercise program.

4. Is home physiotherapy suitable for elderly patients?

Yes. Geriatric home physiotherapy avoids tiring clinic travel and focuses safely on walking balance, joint strength, functional independence, and fall prevention.
