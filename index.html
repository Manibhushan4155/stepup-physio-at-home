FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Step Up Physio At Home?

Step Up Physio At Home is a professional home-visit physiotherapy service led by Dr. Shashi Bhushan Kumar (PT), providing personalized, evidence-based rehabilitation in the comfort of your home across Noida and Delhi.

2. What conditions do you treat?

We treat Neck Pain, Back Pain, Knee Pain, Shoulder Pain, Frozen Shoulder, Postoperative Rehabilitation (TKR, THR), Orthopaedic injuries, Neurological conditions (Stroke, Parkinsonism), Ergonomic posture issues, and Geriatric mobility problems.

3. How does a home physiotherapy session work?

Dr. Shashi visits your home, assesses your condition and movement, understands your recovery goals, and provides a personalized treatment and exercise program while monitoring your progress throughout.

4. Is home physiotherapy suitable for elderly patients?

Yes. Geriatric home physiotherapy is especially helpful for older adults who have difficulty travelling to a clinic, focusing safely on mobility, strength, balance, functional independence, and fall prevention.

5. How often will I need physiotherapy sessions?

The frequency depends on your condition, recovery goals, and rehabilitation needs. After the initial assessment, we recommend a suitable schedule and adjust it as your progress improves.
